# How to Refine a Scrum Product Backlog

Backlog refinement is the act of keeping the backlog described, estimated, and prioritized so that the Scrum team can operate effectively.

## Estimate

How much effort it will take to finish each item or user story.

Relative estimation methods:

- T-shirt sizes
- Story points

### T-shirt sizes

To get started, the team:

- picks one item on the backlog that seems to be about a medium-sized workload, and calls that a "medium" in the estimate field;
- takes another item and compares it to the medium item, answering: "If that first item was a medium, what size would I give this one?";
- repeats this process on each additional item or user story on the backlog until they are all done.

### Story points

Use the Fibonacci sequence to give points.

The important thing to notice about this sequence is that as the list continues, the numbers spread further apart from each other. Because of this, story points provide more accuracy and specificity than T-shirt sizes.

## Best practices

Regardless of the technique you use:

- Ask the Product Owner questions about the user story or item to make sure there is enough information to estimate it.
- Discuss divergent estimates from different team members so that everyone has a chance to understand how to implement the item.
- Agree on the final T-shirt sizes or points value and capture it in the system.
- If certain items fall into the larger T-shirt size or story point range, discuss whether it makes sense to break them into smaller pieces before moving on.
