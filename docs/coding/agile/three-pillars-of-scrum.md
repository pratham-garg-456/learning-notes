# The 3 Pillars of Scrum

## Transparency

Making the most significant aspects of our work visible to those responsible for the outcome.

## Inspection

Conducting timely checks on the outcome of a sprint goal to detect undesirable variances.

## Adaptation

Continuously searching for ways to adjust our project, product, or processes to minimize any further deviation or issues.

## Practice Questions

??? question "1. Name the three pillars of Scrum."

??? question "2. What does transparency mean?"

    Making the most significant aspects of our work visible to those responsible for the outcome.

??? question "3. What is the difference between inspection and adaptation?"

    Inspection is timely checking of progress toward the sprint goal to detect undesirable variances. Adaptation is adjusting the project, product, or processes to minimize further deviation or issues.
