# The 5 Values of Scrum

The notes keep two examples from the course.

**Example (helping a teammate):**

- Putting their own work aside to step in and help a teammate learn the technology.

**Example (courage):**

Courage to:

- take on a hard task,
- ask for help,
- call out negative behavior.
