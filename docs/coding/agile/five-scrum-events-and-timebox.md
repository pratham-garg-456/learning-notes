# The Five Scrum Events and the Timebox

## Five Events in a Scrum

1. The Sprint 
2. Sprint Planning
3. Daily Scrum
4. Sprint Review
5. Sprint Retrospection

## Timebox

The length of sprint

Renges between 1 to 4 weeks

### How to choose?

- Think about frequency of change in the requirements,
    
    Example: New request every week than sprint length for 1 week
    
- How much time developer need to build the item
    
    Example: if it takes one week to create something valuable, then 2 week
    
- How much overhead goes into delivery of the product
    
    Example: If deliverable require a large review with many stakeholders and goes through rigorous testing and quality assurance process then choose a longer sprint such as 3 or 4 weeks

## Practice Questions

??? question "1. Name the five events in Scrum."

    The Sprint, Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective.

??? question "2. What is the timebox, and how long can it be?"

    The length of the sprint, ranging from 1 to 4 weeks.

??? question "3. How do you choose the sprint length?"

    Think about how often requirements change (a new request every week suggests a 1-week sprint), how long developers need to build something valuable, and how much overhead goes into delivery (heavy reviews and QA suggest 3 or 4 weeks).
