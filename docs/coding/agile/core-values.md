# The 4 Core Values of the Agile Manifesto

## 1. Individuals and interactions

This value focuses on people communicating with each other, and values individual perspective and creativity.

## 2. Working software

It is more important to deliver the product the customer wants than to comprehensively document the process you used.

## 3. Customer collaboration

If it isn't valuable to the customer, there is little point spending time on it.

Agile teams are encouraged to seek out every opportunity to include the customer or stakeholder during project execution. This could be:

- presenting early prototypes,
- asking questions, or
- bringing them in to do some initial product testing.

## 4. Responding to change

This value stresses that every Agile team needs to acknowledge that change is inevitable. The most successful projects are the ones that are able to smoothly integrate change.
