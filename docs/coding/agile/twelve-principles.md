# The 12 Principles of the Agile Manifesto

The principles are grouped into themes. The notes keep the examples for four of these themes.

## Theme: focus on what matters

Examples:

- Ensuring the team only works on approved features and doesn't spend time on unnecessary ones.
- Prioritizing getting feedback on a product prototype, so you know which features really matter.
- Reserving 10% of the team's time to work on bug fixing or polishing a process, which should help you go faster in future iterations.

## Theme: business and developers work together

The goal is to enable easy access between business people and developers.

Examples:

- Making sure that business people work near the development team, ideally in the same office or virtual space.
- If that's not possible, maybe co-locating a day a week, encouraging instant messaging, or blocking off time on team calendars each day or week to collaborate.

## Theme: an effective team culture

This theme emphasizes creating an effective team culture that is inclusive, supportive, and empowering. The team:

- is motivated,
- feels trusted,
- has the resources, and
- works at a sustainable pace.

Examples:

- Letting teams write their own processes and templates, rather than forcing them to use something from headquarters.
- Asking the team what kind of equipment they need to do their job, and then giving them those tools.

## Theme: regular reflection

Example:

- Teams should always be figuring out better ways to work, and it's valuable to set time aside after each iteration to focus entirely on how to improve.

See [Agile Methodology](agile-methodology.md) for the full list of twelve principles.

## Practice Questions

??? question "1. How can a team make sure it only works on what matters?"

    Only work on approved features, get early feedback on a prototype to learn which features matter, and reserve about 10% of the team's time for bug fixing or process polishing.

??? question "2. How do you make business people and developers work together?"

    Have business people work near the team (same office or virtual space), or co-locate a day a week, encourage instant messaging, or block off time on calendars each day or week to collaborate.

??? question "3. What makes an effective team culture?"

    A team that is motivated, feels trusted, has the resources it needs, and works at a sustainable pace. For example, let teams write their own processes and templates, and ask what equipment they need.

??? question "4. Why set aside time after each iteration?"

    Teams should always be figuring out better ways to work, so time after each iteration is used entirely to focus on how to improve.
