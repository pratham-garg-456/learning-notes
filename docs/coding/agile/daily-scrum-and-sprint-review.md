# Daily Scrum and Sprint Review

## Daily Scrum (daily stand-up)

15 minutes every day. Each team member answers:

1. What did I do yesterday that helped the Development Team meet the Sprint goal?
2. What will I do today to help the Development Team meet the Sprint goal?
3. Do I notice any impediment that prevents me or the Development Team from meeting our goals?

## Sprint Review

Not more than 4 hours.

A meeting with the entire Scrum Team where the product is demonstrated in order to determine which aspects are finished and which aren't.

- An exploration of which items should be considered done in the Product Backlog.
- They demonstrate and inspect the product.

This leads to the **Product Increment**.

The **Product Increment** is what is produced after a given Sprint and is considered releasable. It is considered releasable when the team has developed a **Minimum Viable Product**, which has a set of implemented features or requirements.

### How do you know a solution is shippable or releasable?

In a Scrum Team, it is ultimately the decision of the **Product Owner** to ensure there is value before releasing an item. To determine this, they may consider:

1. Is the increment complete?
2. Will it bring value and does it meet quality measures? Has it been well tested?
3. Is it usable by the end user? Can we use their direct or indirect feedback to improve future versions of the product?

### Difference between an MVP and a Product Increment

A **minimum viable product** is a package of features that may take several sprints to develop, but every sprint's goal is to produce a **product increment**.
