# IELTS

Notes for the IELTS writing and speaking sections.

In the sample texts below, **bold** marks a word or phrase that was flagged as a mistake in review.

## Writing Task 1 (letter)

Steps:

1. Read the instructions.
2. Identify the style (formal or informal).
3. Read the points.
4. Decide which paragraph each point goes in.
5. Choose the grammar and vocabulary for that letter style.
6. Decide how to sign off.
7. Plan the content for each paragraph.
8. Start writing.

### Structure

- Why you are writing this letter
- First point
- Second point
- Third point
- Sign off
- Your name

### Informal letters

```text
Dear Emily,

.....

Best Wishes

Sarah
```

```text
Hi Dave,

.....

Write back soon,

Sarah
```

```text
Hi John and Laura,

How are you both? I hope this letter finds you well. Although it's such a short time since we last saw each other, it feels much longer.

Sorry, it has taken me so long to write back and thank you.

Take care and write back soon.

Sandra
```

### Formal letters

!!! warning
    Formal letters must not use contractions like I'd or it's.

```text
Dear John,

I am writing to ..

Best Regards,

Richard Brollend
```

```text
Dear Mr Drummond,

I am writing to ..

Yours sincerely,

Richard Brollend
```

```text
Dear Sir,

I am writing this letter to ..

Yours faithfully,

Richard Brollend
```

```text
Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing this letter to ..

Yours faithfully,

Richard Brollend
```

Rule of thumb from these examples: use "Yours sincerely" when you know the name (Dear Mr Drummond), and "Yours faithfully" when you don't (Dear Sir or Madam).

## Writing Task 2 (essay)

Five kinds of essay:

1. **Opinion essay** (agree or disagree)
2. **Discussion essay** (discuss both views)
3. **Problem-solution essay**
4. **Advantage/disadvantage essay**
5. **Direct/question essay**

### Paragraph pattern

1. **Topic sentence (main idea):** Home education allows children to explore subjects at their own pace.
2. **Supporting details:** Rather than being confined by a rigid curriculum, children can focus on areas of personal interest, such as music or science.
3. **Example or evidence:** For instance, a child passionate about music could dedicate more time to mastering an instrument instead of following a standardized school program.
4. **Concluding sentence:** This personalized approach helps cultivate genuine passion and expertise in chosen fields.

### General structure

**Introduction**

- Background statement: paraphrase the statement.
- Thesis statement: your answer (an introduction to your ideas).

Tips:

- Don't use "like"; use "such as".
- Add a clause to extend a sentence:
    - Building wider roads can solve traffic congestion because it allows traffic to move more easily.
    - Building wider roads can solve traffic congestion, which is a serious problem in major cities during rush hours, because it allows traffic to move more easily.
    - Investing in renewable energy can decrease national reliance on fossil fuels, a challenge that many governments are eager to overcome, since it reduces harmful emissions and promotes sustainability.
    - Adopting a balanced diet can enhance overall health, a practice recommended by nutrition experts, because it supplies essential nutrients, while reducing the risk of chronic illnesses.
- Conclusion openers: "In conclusion", "To conclude".

### Opinion essay

Choose a clear position (fully agree, fully disagree, or partially agree or disagree) and support your view with reasons and examples.

**Some people say that it is better to work for a large company than a small one. Do you agree or disagree?**

> Job seekers always struggle to pick between a small company and a large company when applying for jobs. Working at a large company often comes with more perks and benefits, and a small company gives you more opportunity to self-learn. In this essay, I will examine how working at a big company has more benefits than a small one.

Plan for the body:

- Large company advantages: high salary; job stability as the company is more financially stable; extra perks like gym, refreshments, and bonuses; opportunities for professional development through mentoring and coaching.
- Large company disadvantages: big management.

**The best way to improve health is to do regular exercise. To what extent do you agree?**

> The most effective way of developing and improving health is considered to be daily exercise.
>
> Daily exercise is considered one of the most effective ways to improve health. In my opinion, I agree that exercise is the key to health. However, I also believe that diet is important.

**The growing number of overweight people is putting a strain on the health care system in an effort to deal with the health issues involved. Some people think that the best way to deal with this problem is to introduce more physical education lessons in the school curriculum. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

> The measures to tackle health problems, which is caused by increasing number of overweight population, is putting strain on the health care system. It is commonly thought that introducing physical education in the school curriculum is the most effective way. I agree that this is one of the ways to tackle the problem but diet must also be taken into consideration.

**Introduction rewrite (before and after)**

Before:

> **According to majority of the people** public sectors such as healthcare and education **should be supported more than the infrastructure development financially**. While**,** it is true that health and education are the **building block**s of the society, **we should not forget that** infrastructure **development** also c**ome hand in hand with the development of the society**. In this essay, **we** will **look** how both **sides work together to build a prospering community.**

After:

> It's widely believed that public sectors such as healthcare and education should receive greater financial support than infrastructure development. While it is true that public sectors like health and education are the cornerstones of society, infrastructure also plays a crucial role in fostering overall growth. In this essay, I will examine how both aspects are interdependent and contribute to building a thriving community.

### Discussion essay

Phrases for introducing views:

- Many people believe that ...
- It is often believed that ...
- Many argue that ...
- ... throw the balance in favour of ...
- It is considered by some that ..., while there are others ...

**Question:** Completing university education is thought by some to be the best way to get a good job. On the other hand, other people think that getting experience and developing soft skills is more important. Discuss both sides and give your opinion.

### Problem-solution essay

**Many offenders commit more crimes after serving their first punishment. Why is this happening, and what measures can be taken to tackle this problem?**

Before:

> It is observed that people who **has** broken the law tend to **it** more often after serving their first punishment. The reason behind this can be lack of support services aimed at rehabilitation and reintegration into the society. In this essay, we will take a look at some steps, which can be incorporated into the **current law system**, to minimize this issue.

Introduction after:

> It is observed that individuals who have broken the law tend to reoffend more often after serving their first punishment. This is mainly due to lack of support services aimed at rehabilitation and reintegration into society. In this essay, we will take a look at some steps that can be incorporated into the criminal justice system to address this issue.

Structure:

- **Body paragraph 1:** describe the reason.
- **Body paragraph 2:** give solutions (two minimum).
- **Conclusion:** give the reason and solution in one line.

### Advantage/disadvantage essay

Use language about advantages and disadvantages:

- drawback
- benefits
- downside
- advantageous
- evident benefit
- In other words,
- Another reason
- hardly
- appealed
- things have moved on since then
- escapism
- healthier pursuits
- constructive
- sedentary
- In terms of why

**Question:** The development of tourism contributed to English becoming the most prominent language in the world. Some people think this will lead to English becoming the only language to be spoken globally. What are the advantages and disadvantages of having one language in the world?

Sentence starter: "It is thought by some people that ... may one day ..."

### Direct/question essay

**More and more adults are playing computer games. Why is this happening? Is it a positive or negative trend?**

Why? The education system and society give computer games the same level of encouragement as outdoor games.

## Speaking

1. Topic and ideas
2. Word list
3. Record your answer
4. Listen and check:
    1. Fluency
    2. Vocabulary
    3. Grammar
    4. Pronunciation
5. Practice and record again

## Practice Questions

??? question "1. What are the eight steps for planning a Writing Task 1 letter?"

    Read the instructions, identify the style, read the points, decide which paragraph each point goes in, choose grammar and vocabulary for that style, decide the sign-off, plan each paragraph, and start writing.

??? question "2. When do you use Yours sincerely and when Yours faithfully?"

    Yours sincerely when you know the name (Dear Mr Drummond). Yours faithfully when you don't (Dear Sir or Madam).

??? question "3. What is the key rule about contractions in formal letters?"

    Formal letters must not use contractions like I'd or it's.

??? question "4. Name the five kinds of Task 2 essay."

    Opinion (agree or disagree), discussion (both views), problem-solution, advantage/disadvantage, and direct/question.

??? question "5. What are the four parts of a good paragraph?"

    Topic sentence (main idea), supporting details, example or evidence, and concluding sentence.

??? question "6. What does an essay introduction contain?"

    A background statement that paraphrases the question, then a thesis statement that answers it and introduces your ideas.

??? question "7. How can you extend a simple sentence?"

    Add a clause, for example: "Building wider roads can solve traffic congestion, which is a serious problem in major cities during rush hours, because it allows traffic to move more easily."

??? question "8. What is the structure of a problem-solution essay?"

    An introduction, a body paragraph describing the reason, a body paragraph giving at least two solutions, and a conclusion that gives the reason and solution in one line.

??? question "9. What are the five steps of the speaking practice routine?"

    Choose a topic and ideas, make a word list, record your answer, listen and check fluency, vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation, then practice and record again.
